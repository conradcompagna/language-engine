Privacy policy

Last updated May 11, 2026.

Information collected. Language Engine stores the information needed to run your account, including your email address, login method, subscription status, Stripe billing identifiers, usage counters, support messages, uploaded documents or text, lookup activity, custom entries, and annotations.

How it is used. We use account and usage data to provide the reader, dictionary lookup, NLP analysis, AI features, billing, support, security, and account-management tools.

Service providers. Payments are processed by Stripe, Google sign-in is handled by Google, account emails are sent through the configured email provider, and AI features may send the text needed for a request to the configured AI provider.

Sharing. We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to operate the app, when required by law, or when needed to protect the service from abuse.

Your choices. You can change your password, manage billing, or delete your login and billing account from this page. For other privacy questions, use the Contact link.

Terms and cancellation policy

Accounts. You are responsible for keeping your account email current and for protecting access to your account.

Uploaded content. You are responsible for the documents and text you upload or process with the app, including having the right to use that material.

Service output. Dictionary data, NLP analysis, translations, and AI-generated output can contain mistakes. Check important results before relying on them.

Subscriptions. Paid plans are billed by Stripe at the monthly or yearly price shown at checkout. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled.

Cancellation. You can manage or cancel a subscription with the Manage Subscription button above. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Payments are not prorated or refunded unless required by law or approved by Language Engine.

Email. Language Engine may send account recovery, billing, and service-related emails.

Termination. Access may be suspended or ended for nonpayment, abuse, unlawful use, or attempts to interfere with the service.

Contact. Use the Contact link for questions about these terms.
